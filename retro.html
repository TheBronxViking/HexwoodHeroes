HEXWOOD HEROES

A SPELL-SLINGING QUEST

PLAYERS
PLAYER 1 — W A S D  ·  F CAST  ·  G JUMP  ·  R SLIDE  ·  E SHIELD
PLAYER 2 — ARROWS  ·  , CAST  ·  . JUMP  ·  / SLIDE  ·  R-SHIFT SHIELD
ONE PLAYERArrows or WASD to move — Z or F to cast — X or G to jump — C or R to slide
AIMINGHold UP or DOWN while casting to fire diagonally or straight up
SHIELDHold V (or E) to raise a force field — blocks all bullets, drains and recharges
DODGINGYou can't be hit while sliding — slide straight through a barrage
FLOATKeep JUMP held near the top of a jump to hang in the air
PLATFORMSDOWN + JUMP drops you through a wooden platform
GAMEPADSPlug in one or two — they take over automatically
OTHERESC pauses — M mutes

Original game. Character models: KayKit Adventurers by Kay Lousberg (CC0).
